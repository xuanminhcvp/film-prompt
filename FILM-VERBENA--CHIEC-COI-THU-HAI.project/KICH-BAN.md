## HOOK

"WHERE DID YOU GET THAT?" Desmond Thorne crossed the lobby so fast his coffee went over the rim.

Verbena Sackett was holding the door for a man in a wheelchair. Her keys were still in her fist. A silver whistle hung from the ring, dented flat on one side, a letter and a number scratched into it.

Thorne took her wrist and turned it over.

"That came out of my house," he said. "Empty your pockets."

Twenty two people waited for the elevators. Two of them raised their phones. Nobody moved.

Verbena did not pull back.

"My mother gave me that," she said. "Thirty one years ago."

He heard the year, not the sentence. He called the police. By seven that morning she had lost the only job that would hire a woman with her record.

He did not know what he was holding. In nine days he would learn what those scratches meant, in front of a full room.

She had carried it since she was nine. She had never once blown it.
