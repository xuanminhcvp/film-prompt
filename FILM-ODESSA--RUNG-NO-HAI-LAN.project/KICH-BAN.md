## HOOK

"Put the bucket down and step outside. This is an engineering discussion."

Callum Reyes said it without turning around. Odessa Pruitt stood behind him with a mop bucket. She had just told him which bell was wrong.

Eleven people in that tower. Two laughed. Not one said her name.

The foundry had sent two men. Both ran a spectrum analyzer across all forty eight bells. Both said every bell was inside tolerance. The dedication was in nine days. Two million dollars of bronze, and it sounded like a truck hitting a guardrail.

Odessa set the bucket down anyway.

"The fourth one is flat on the tierce," she said. "Ring it twice. Listen to what comes after."

Nobody rang anything. Reyes waved her toward the stairs. She went.

What she heard up there, no instrument in the building could measure. She had learned to hear it before Reyes was born.

To understand how, go back forty one years, to a foundry floor in Ohio where a girl was handed a hammer and told to listen.
