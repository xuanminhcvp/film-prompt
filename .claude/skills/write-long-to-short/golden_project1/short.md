# SHORT: THE PAPER CROWN
Nguồn: "No One Remembered the Elderly Billionaire's Birthday" — Scene 20 → 22 → 23
Thời lượng mục tiêu: 2:35 – 2:40  (cold open không thoại 10s / 3 shot + 2:14 thoại + nhịp hành động)

SCENE 20: THE PENTHOUSE DOOR

[SCENE CONTEXT]: Seconds after the knock. Harold thinks it is the maintenance man about the faucet. He does not know anyone remembered the date. Bennett is a maintenance worker in this building and is breaking the rules by bringing his daughter upstairs. Lily is six. She made the crown at school and kept it for somebody who needed one.
[SCENE PURPOSE]: Pay off the silence of the cold open — the person at the door is a child. Break Harold's armor with a paper crown.

HAROLD: The door is open. What is it now? The faucet again?
LILY: Happy birthday.
BENNETT: This is my daughter, Lily. She wanted to meet you, sir.
HAROLD: Who sent you?
BENNETT: No one, sir.
HAROLD: People do not just show up at my door with — is that a cupcake?
LILY: With a candle. I only had one candle, so you have to pretend it is eighty-two.
HAROLD: How did you know?
LILY: My daddy told me it was your birthday. He said nobody remembered.
HAROLD: And you thought a cupcake would fix that?
LILY: I also made you a crown. Everybody deserves a crown on their birthday.

[The crown is paper. Gold glitter, red crayon, one letter spelled wrong. Nobody has given Harold something made by hand since his wife died.]

HAROLD: Nobody makes me anything. Not anymore.
LILY: I know. That is why.
HAROLD: That is why what?
LILY: That is why I made you one.

[Harold puts the crown on his head. His hands are not steady.]

LILY: It looks good on you. You look like a real king. Kings sit at tables with people.
HAROLD: Sit down. Both of you. You are making the room nervous.

[END STATE]: Harold is wearing a child's paper crown and has invited a maintenance worker to his table. His daughter is downstairs in the same building with legal papers.

---

SCENE 22: THE INTERRUPTION

[SCENE CONTEXT]: Victoria Prescott, forty-four, is Harold's daughter. She came today for a signature, not for his birthday. She has not been inside this apartment in eight years. She does not know who is sitting at her father's table.
[SCENE PURPOSE]: Collide the two worlds. Show that a stranger's child gave Harold what Victoria never could.

[Victoria expected to find her father alone with the folder she left on his table.]

VICTORIA: Father. What is this?
HAROLD: A birthday party. You are welcome to stay.
VICTORIA: Who are these people?
HAROLD: This is Bennett. He fixes things in the building. This is Lily. She makes crowns.

[Victoria sees the paper crown on her father's head. Her face is not angry. It is closer to jealousy.]

VICTORIA: You do not know these people.
HAROLD: I did not know the lawyers you sent either. At least these two brought cake.

[Harold has just told his daughter that a stranger's visit meant more than hers.]

[END STATE]: Victoria now sees her father happy in a way she has never made him. Her suspicion is rising and it is covering something else.

---

SCENE 23: THE ACCUSATION

[SCENE CONTEXT]: Same room, moments later. Victoria believes Bennett planned this to get money. Bennett does not know there are papers or a fortune involved. Nora Stanton manages the building and already warned Bennett once today.
[SCENE PURPOSE]: Take everything away from the person who gave the most. End on the cost of doing the right thing.

VICTORIA: Mr. Underwood. How much did my father promise you?
BENNETT: Nothing. He does not know my name.
VICTORIA: He just said your name.
BENNETT: He learned it twenty minutes ago.

[Victoria stops listening and picks up the house phone.]

VICTORIA: Ms. Stanton. The penthouse. Now.
NORA: Underwood?
VICTORIA: Is he allowed up here with a child?
NORA: No, ma'am. I gave him fifteen minutes.
VICTORIA: He used them on an eighty-two-year-old man who is about to sign over control of two billion dollars.
BENNETT: I came here with a cupcake, ma'am. My daughter baked it last night.
HAROLD: I see two people who brought me a cupcake. And I see my daughter who brought me a folder.

[Nobody moves.]

HAROLD: Both of you came to my door today. Only one of you remembered what day it is.
VICTORIA: That is not fair.
HAROLD: No. It is not.

[Victoria has no answer, so she reaches for the only power she has left.]

VICTORIA: Get him out.
NORA: Underwood. Your badge.

[Bennett puts his badge in her hand. Lily is still holding the empty cupcake box.]

LILY: Daddy. Did we do something bad?

[END STATE]: Bennett has lost his job for bringing a cupcake. Harold defended him and it was not enough. The question hangs and nobody answers it.
