"No One Will Save You!" White Man Smashed Black Man's Phone — Then a Pentagon Chopper Landed

"You think you can walk in here? Look at you — a roach in a suit. The dirt's in your DNA, boy."
"Sir, I have an appointment. If you'd just call upstairs —"
"Shut your mouth. I decide who belongs here. Trash like you doesn't deserve to breathe this air."
That was Brock Halstead. Head of security at one of America's biggest defense companies. And the man standing in front of him — calm, quiet, hands at his sides — was someone Halstead had just made the worst mistake of his life talking to.
Wesley Stanton. Polo shirt. Khakis. No badge. He looked like someone's dad on his lunch break. He was on the phone with his wife when Halstead walked up.
Halstead snatched the phone and smashed it on the marble floor. Glass everywhere. Thirty employees froze. Nobody said a word.
He stepped closer. "Ain't nobody saving your Black ass. Sit down before I drag you out like the dog you are."
Wesley looked at the shattered glass. Then up at Halstead. No anger. No fear. Just pity.
"You just ended your career."
Halstead laughed. Snapped at two guards. They sat Wesley down. Searched his briefcase — Pentagon documents, a classified laptop, a government badge with a clearance level none of them had ever seen.
Halstead tossed it aside. "Fake."
Then Wesley pulled out a second phone. Matte black. No logo. He dialed one number.
"This is Stanton. Activate Protocol Overwatch."
Three minutes later, the building shook. A military helicopter landed on the rooftop. The executive elevator opened. A colonel in full dress uniform stepped out with two armed officers.
She walked past every frozen employee. Straight to Wesley.
"Mr. Stanton. Are you alright?"
Then she turned to Halstead. And what came out of her mouth drained every drop of color from his face.
But what she said — and what happened to Halstead next — that's a story you're not ready for.
