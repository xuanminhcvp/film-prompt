Flight Attendant Yelled at Black Boy “No Food for You” — Didn’t Know His Father Owned Entire Airline

"Get your dirty hands off that menu. No food for you."
"My father paid for this seat."
"Your father? Your daddy couldn't afford the luggage rack up here."
"Here's my boarding pass. Seat 2A."
"I don't care what that says. You stink up my cabin. Get out."
"No ma'am. I belong here."
"Shut your mouth and get to economy before I call security on you."
Eleven years old. Gray hoodie. Scuffed sneakers. The boy didn't flinch. Didn't blink. 
The flight attendant — fifteen years seniority, blond hair pulled tight — had poured champagne for the white couple in row one. Warm smiles for the businessman in the suit.
For this boy? Not a glance. Not a word. Just venom.
A man in row 3 leaned out. Whiskey breath. Expensive watch. "She's right, kid. Back in my day, people like you rode in the cargo hold."
Nobody else said a word.
Then she grabbed his arm. Marched him down the aisle. Past every row. Past every silent face. All the way to 34B. Worst seat on the plane.
No food. No phone. No one.
When the meal cart came, she served his left. Served his right. Reached across him like he was furniture.
Skipped him.
"We've run out." Smiling.
But six trays still on the cart.
Three hours. Hungry. Alone.
The plane landed.
He walked off the jet bridge — and froze.
His father stood at the gate.
Tall. Tailored coat. Eyes like iron. Beside him, the VP of Operations. Tablet in hand. Every record pulled up.
She strolled out. Rolling her bag. Adjusting her scarf.
"Ms. Holloway. This is Derek Fletcher."
She blinked.
"Founder and sole owner of SkyVault Airlines."
Her bag hit the floor.
"And that boy you just dragged off — is his son."
Silence.
"Sir — if I had known —"
Derek raised one hand.
"That… is exactly the problem."
One nod.
"Your badge. Now."
Fifteen years. Gone in fifteen seconds.
The footage went viral. Twenty-eight million views.
