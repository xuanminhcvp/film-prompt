Neighbor Smashes Black Man's Gas Station Window Over Free Gas—Cops She Calls Arrest Her on His Order

"You filthy, disgusting cockroach. You think you can stand behind that counter and tell ME to pay? Look at you. You smell like you crawled out of a sewer."
"Ma'am, the total is forty-two sixteen. Same price for everyone."
"Everyone? I'm not EVERYONE. I've lived here thirty-one years. You're nothing. You're a stain on this neighborhood. You should be grateful I even walk through that door."
"I understand, ma'am. But I still need you to pay for your gas."
"Pay? I'd rather burn this place down than give a single cent to someone like YOU."
That was Brenda. Talking to the quiet Black man who bought the gas station two years ago. The old owner used to give her free fill-ups. New owner said no. And Brenda decided he needed to learn his place.
She came back three times in two days. Demanded free gas. Lied to the teenage cashier. Smashed a display off the counter. And the third time, she grabbed a brick and launched it through the front window. Glass exploded everywhere. The nineteen-year-old cashier hit the floor bleeding.
Then Brenda called 911.
"There's an aggressive Black man here. He attacked me. He grabbed me. Send someone NOW."
The man was standing twenty feet away. Arms folded. Silent. His phone recording every second.
Four patrol cars pulled in. Brenda ran to the first officer.
"ARREST HIM! He attacked me!"
The officer looked at the shattered glass. Looked at the brick. Then looked at the man by the pumps.
And froze.
His spine went straight. His hand snapped to the brim of his hat.
"Chief Wilson? Sir?"
The man she called a cockroach. The man she told to crawl back where he came from. He was the retired Police Chief of the entire county. Twelve years commanding every officer on that lot. He trained them. Pinned their badges on personally.
Grant picked up his phone. Called the current Chief — the man he mentored and hand-picked as his replacement.
"Full charges. By the book."
Brenda was cuffed in the same lot where she threw that brick. By the officers she called. On the order of the man she tried to destroy.
