## HOOK — THE EMPTY TABLE
*[SCENE CONTEXT]: Flash-forward. A penthouse dining table set for one. An eighty-two-year-old man sits alone. Then a knock. The audience does not know who these people are yet. [SCENE PURPOSE]: Hook the audience instantly. Establish the thesis line. Create Open Loop.*

## CTA
Sometimes the richest man in the room is the poorest man at the table. Wealth can build walls so high that even family can not see over them. Bennett and Lily did not give Harold his fortune — they gave him something no fortune could buy. A paper crown. A crayon drawing. Proof that someone remembered, someone showed up, someone looked. And in the end, that was more than enough.
