🎬 A Billionaire Asked a Waitress for Stock Tips to Mock Her — Then He Started Taking Notes

Tuesday, eight-fifteen a.m., Fulton Street Grill. Billionaire Willard Croft, sixty-one, asks waitress Frankie Tate, thirty-four, for a stock tip as a joke. She names his fund's own position and tells him to sell. Eleven days later that company drops forty percent before lunch — and his head of research gets walked off the forty-first floor for faking his fieldwork.

Nine years of notebooks, filled with what warehouse crews and drivers let slip over coffee, just outperformed a two-million-dollar research budget. Not a lucky guess — a method, every miss written in the same ink as every hit.

✔️ "Triple-source or it doesn't count."
The line that separates a discipline from a hunch — and from the analyst caught faking his.
